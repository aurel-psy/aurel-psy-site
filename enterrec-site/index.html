Booking & Contact

Whether it's booking, a demo submission or a general enquiry — we'd love to hear from you.

🎧 DJ / Artist Booking

Looking to book Champa, Aurel or one of our Crew for your event or festival? Reach out with your event details.

🎵 Music Submission

We accept demos only via private SoundCloud links. Please don't send downloadable files — only finished tracks that fit our label's sound.

📬 Mailing List

Want first access to new releases and news? Ask to be added to the Enterrec mailing list.

💬 General Enquiry

Press, collabs, licensing or anything else — send us a message and we'll get back to you.

For all enquiries, email us directly:

enterrecinfo@gmail.com
