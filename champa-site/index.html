The Story

About Champa

From vinyl at age two to label boss — a life dedicated to psychedelic trance.

UK-born DJ and producer Champa, a.k.a. DJ Prozak, has been a major force in the underground dance scene for nearly three decades as a DJ and over 13 years as a producer. Known for his dynamic and full-power psytrance sets, he has consistently wowed crowds around the world.

In recent years, Champa has perfected his production craft, solidifying his place as one of the leading artists in the global psytrance community. His music is a blend of groovy basslines, infectious percussion, and relentless, driving melodies — designed to be played loud and danced to with pure joy. His live performances are high-energy, uplifting, and crafted to unite audiences with a shared passion for the psytrance experience.

With an unmistakable signature sound, Champa continues to push the boundaries of the genre, creating music that resonates with a pan-global audience.

"My whole life has been dedicated to being involved with music. I began to learn to play vinyl when I was 2 years old and started to learn to be a DJ when I turned 16."

After moving through Jungle, Garage, Hard Trance, Hard House, German Techno, Happy Hardcore and Progressive House, Champa found his home in Full-On Psy-Trance in 2005. In 2011, inspired by the birth of his son Vinnie, he began producing — releasing first through Y.S.E. Recordings, then taking on A&R duties for the GOA Series compilations. In June 2017, after nearly joining another label, he took the leap and founded Enterrec — building his own family of psychedelic producers and DJs to push the scene further.
